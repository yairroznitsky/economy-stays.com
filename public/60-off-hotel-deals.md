Hotel deals on Cheap Stays means comparing rates hotels currently list — including properties that show a promotional price. We do not set markdowns or promise a fixed percentage off. Search a city, then read the offer details on the booking site.

## Hotel deals vs a fixed markdown

Ads and sitelinks often use a striking discount line because travelers scan for a deal. On this page, the honest version is: some hotels show a promotional rate next to a usual one, and some do not. Cheap Stays does not choose the markdown and does not guarantee a percentage off.

When a hotel lists a deal, you can see it after you search a destination and open the offer. The conditions — dates, room type, cancellation — are on that site, with the hotel.

## How promotional hotel rates show up

Run a search for your city and dates. Tomorrow is only a default so the form is ready. Compare the stays that come back. If a property is promoting a rate, that can appear in the comparison or on the page you continue to.

A promotion for next month may not apply this weekend. If you can flex dates, try a second search. If you cannot, read the offer that actually matches your nights.

## Read the deal where you book

Headline savings are easy to misunderstand. Check whether the rate is prepaid, whether breakfast is included, and whether you can cancel. A marked-down stay with no flexibility can still be a good fit — if you know that before you pay.

Cheap Stays does not invent crossed-out prices. Anything that looks like a discount should be confirmed on the booking site.

## Timing a hotel-deal search

Deal density changes with season, events, and how full a hotel is. Searching early can surface advance-purchase rates. Searching closer to arrival can surface a different mix. Neither approach is “the trick”; they are just different snapshots.

Use the destination cards to sample popular cities, or open a city landing if you want a broader guide before you compare.

## How to compare hotel deals

1. Search your destination and set real travel dates.
2. Compare hotels and rentals that are listed — including any promotional rates they show.
3. Open the offer on the booking site and read conditions, dates, and cancellation rules.
4. Book on that site only if the deal still matches the stay you want.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is every hotel on sale?

No. You’ll see a mix of standard and promotional rates. Check the offer details on the booking site before you book.

### Do you choose the markdown?

No. Any discount is between the hotel and the booking site. We don’t set or promise a percentage off.

### How do I find a stronger current rate?

Enter your destination and dates, compare the options, then sort or filter on the booking site.

### Do deals apply to every room type?

Often they do not. A promotion may be limited to certain rooms or dates. The offer spells that out.

### Can I combine a deal with other coupons?

That depends on the booking site. Cheap Stays does not issue coupons or stack codes.

### Why did a deal disappear when I clicked through?

Promotional inventory can be limited. If it is gone, compare other listings or try nearby dates.
